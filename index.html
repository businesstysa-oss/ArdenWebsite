What We Do

Every surface,
handled

From the first coat to the final detail — we handle the full range of painting and finishing work for homes and businesses across Denver.

Interior Painting

Professional interior painting for walls, ceilings, trim, doors, and accent features. We combine careful preparation, crisp lines, and durable finishes that hold up to everyday living.

Exterior Painting & Staining

Protect and refresh your home's exterior with premium paints and stains designed for Colorado's climate. We refresh siding, trim, decks, and fences with careful preparation and coatings built to withstand sun, storms, and seasonal wear.

Cabinet & Trim Finishes

Cabinet and trim refinishing that transforms kitchens, built-ins, doors, and woodwork without the cost of full replacement. Available in painted, stained, and sealed finishes designed to look clean, custom, and built to last.

Drywall Repair

Seamless drywall and plaster repair for holes, cracks, water damage, patches, and texture issues. We properly repair surfaces before painting so the finished result looks complete, not covered up.

Commercial Painting

Professional painting services for offices, retail spaces, open ceilings, tenant improvements, and new construction projects. We work efficiently, communicate clearly, and keep projects moving with minimal disruption.

Specialty Finishes & Wallpaper

Specialty finishes including wallpaper installation, epoxy floor coatings, and decorative treatments designed to bring texture, character, and distinction to a space.

Our Process

How we work,
from first call to final walkthrough

Every project follows the same disciplined approach — because good results aren't an accident, they're built step by step.

01

Consultation

We start with the space, goals, surfaces, timeline, and finish expectations so the scope is clear before work begins.

02

Finish Planning

Complimentary color consultations with a Paint Designer to help select the right colors, sheen, and finishes for your space.

03

Preparation

Protection, repair, sanding, masking, caulking, and priming are completed with the final finish in mind.

04

Application

We apply the selected finish system with care, consistency, and methods appropriate for the surface.

05

Final Refinement

We complete touch-ups, cleanup, inspection, and walkthrough so the finished space feels complete and considered.

Who We Are
